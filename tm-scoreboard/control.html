Scoreboard control

Use this page on your laptop. Put projector on the TV or second screen and drag that browser tab there.

What shows on the projector

Changing this updates the projector right away.

Message for projector

When “White screen — typed message” is selected, this text fills the projector on a white background (black type). It scales automatically for legibility from about 10 feet on a typical full-screen window.

Scores

Change numbers here, then press Update scores on projector to run the sliding animation.

Timer

This switches the projector to the timer, loads the length, shows it for half a second, then starts the countdown. Length also applies when you reset while paused.

Synced via your browser. Keep both tabs open on this computer.
