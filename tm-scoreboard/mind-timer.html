Mental timer

Open this page in its own window while you run an activity. Participants count silently to your target time (for example one minute) and raise a finger when they think they are there. Use Space to start and pause the stopwatch. Press M, Z, or D when each person raises a finger to record their elapsed time and how early or late they were versus the target you enter below.

Target time

Delta = elapsed − target. Negative means they raised early; positive means late.
